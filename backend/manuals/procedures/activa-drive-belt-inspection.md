---
title: Drive belt inspection (Activa)
make: Honda
models: [Activa, Activa 6G]
kind: procedure
---
Team-written general steps, check against the workshop manual.

# Open the CVT cover
1. Put the scooter on the main stand with the engine cold.
2. Remove the left-side CVT cover bolts and take the cover off. Keep the gasket or seal clean.

# Inspect the belt
3. Look along the whole belt: cracks, missing pieces, glazing or fraying mean it needs replacing.
4. Measure the belt width and compare with the service limit in the workshop manual.
5. Check the pulleys and rollers for wear and the inside of the cover for belt dust.

# Close up
6. Clean out the belt dust, refit the cover and tighten the bolts to the workshop-manual value.
7. Test ride: smooth pick-up, no slipping or juddering.
