Team-written general steps, check against the workshop manual.

# Remove
1. Let the engine cool. Pull the plug cap off by the cap, not the wire.
2. Clean around the plug base so dirt can't fall into the cylinder, then unscrew it with a plug spanner.

# Inspect
3. Look at the tip: tan or light grey is normal; black and sooty, oily, or white and blistered means something else needs checking.
4. Check the electrode for wear and the insulator for cracks. Replace with the plug type in the owner's manual.
5. Check the gap with a feeler gauge against the owner's manual value; adjust by bending the side electrode only.

# Refit
6. Screw the plug in by hand first to avoid cross-threading, then tighten to the workshop-manual value.
7. Refit the cap firmly and start the engine.
