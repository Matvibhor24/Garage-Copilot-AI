title: Clutch plate replacement (Splendor)
make: Hero
models: [Splendor, Splendor Plus]

Team-written general steps, check against the workshop manual.

# Before you start
Park the bike on the main stand on level ground with the engine cold. Keep a tray for the bolts and a new
clutch plate set and gasket ready. All tightening values and clearances come from the workshop manual.

# Drain and open
1. Drain the engine oil into a tray.
2. Remove the kick-start lever and the right-side footrest if they are in the way.
3. Loosen the right crankcase cover bolts in a criss-cross pattern and lift the cover off. Keep the gasket face clean.

# Take out the old plates
4. Loosen the clutch spring bolts a little at a time in a criss-cross pattern, then remove the springs and the pressure plate.
5. Pull out the friction plates and steel plates together, keeping their order.
6. Check the clutch housing and hub slots for grooves. Worn slots make a new clutch drag; show the owner if you find them.

# Fit the new plates
7. Soak the new friction plates in fresh engine oil before fitting.
8. Fit friction and steel plates in the same order as they came out.
9. Refit the pressure plate and springs; tighten the bolts in a criss-cross pattern to the workshop-manual value.

# Close up and check
10. Fit a new cover gasket, refit the cover and tighten the bolts in a criss-cross pattern.
11. Refill engine oil of the grade and quantity in the owner's manual.
12. Set the clutch lever free play to the owner's manual value, then test ride: clean take-off, no slipping under load.
