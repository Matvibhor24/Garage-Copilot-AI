Team-written general steps, check against the workshop manual.

# Check the slack
1. Put the bike on the main stand, gearbox in neutral, engine off.
2. Measure the up-and-down movement of the chain midway between the sprockets. Compare with the slack value in the owner's manual.
3. Turn the wheel and check at a few points: adjust at the tightest point.

# Adjust
4. Loosen the rear axle nut.
5. Turn both adjusters by the same amount so the wheel stays in line; the marks on both sides must match.
6. Tighten the axle nut to the workshop-manual value and recheck the slack.

# Finish
7. Clean and lubricate the chain. Check the rear brake free play after moving the wheel.
8. If the chain has tight spots or the sprocket teeth are hooked, tell the owner the chain set needs replacing.
